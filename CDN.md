## Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

### Definition
A **CDN** is a network of interconnected servers spread globally to deliver web content quickly to users based on their geographic location.

### Core Benefits
* **Speed:** Reduces latency by serving content from a "nearby" server.
* **Reliability:** High availability ensures content is reachable even during traffic spikes.
* **Affordability:** Scalable rental models (like CloudFront or Akamai) are cheaper than maintaining global hardware.
* **Control:** Offers pluggable algorithms to manage how data is fetched or invalidated (cleared).

### Popular Providers
* **Amazon CloudFront**
* **Akamai**
* **Cloudflare**
